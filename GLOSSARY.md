# Glossary: DRAM Deep Trench Capacitor Etch

Terms are defined as they are used in this book. Chapter references point to the main discussion.

---

## A

**A (aspect ratio):** Depth over width, h/w. The reference trench is 71:1 at the top minor CD (5000/70) and 83:1 at the average minor CD (5000/60). (Ch. 1.3.2)

**A_tot (total aspect ratio):** (h_mask + h)/w, including remaining BSG, pad SiN, and pad oxide; ≈ 91 at the end of R1. (Ch. 1.3.2, 4.1.2)

**Acceptance angle:** Largest ion angle that reaches the bottom without striking the wall; arctan(w/h_tot); 0.53° at the end of R1. (Ch. 4.1.2)

**APC (advanced process control):** Feed-forward of CD and BSG plus EWMA feedback on ER₀ to set the main-etch time. (Ch. 15)

**ARDE (aspect-ratio-dependent etching):** Fall of the bottom rate with depth; ER = ER₀/(1 + kA). (Ch. 4)

**ARDE coefficient (k):** 0.020 for R1, 0.012 for R2 (w̄ = 60 nm); k ≈ (3/4)s_eff. (Ch. 4.3.3)

---

## B

**Black silicon:** Dense needles formed by micromasking; the window edge of cryogenic etch. (Ch. 7.4.1, 12.1.3)

**Bosch process:** Time-multiplexed SF₆ etch / C₄F₈ passivation; used for wide trench capacitors and TSVs, not for the 70 nm trench because of scallops. (Ch. 8.4.1)

**Bottle:** Lower trench (1.20–4.80 µm) widened by 25 nm per side after the collar is formed; adds 61% to the capacitance. (Ch. 1.4.2, 13)

**Bow:** Widening of the upper trench below the pad films from facet reflections, mask charging, broad ions, and early thin film; ≤ 5 nm spec, ≈ 1–2 nm reference. (Ch. 10.2)

**BOX (buried oxide):** 145 nm oxide in the SOI stack; requires a fluorocarbon step and can cause a notch. (Ch. 14.1)

**BSG (borosilicate glass):** Reference hard mask, 900 nm; strips in HF 20–50× faster than thermal oxide. (Ch. 2.1.1)

**BT (breakthrough):** 10 s fluorine-rich, high-bias step that clears native oxide and residue. (Ch. 2.5, 8.2)

**Buried plate:** Arsenic-doped silicon around the bottle; the counter-electrode, tied to the N-band. (Ch. 1.2.1)

**Buried strap:** Doped poly contact from the top of the storage node to the access transistor. (Ch. 1.2.1, 14.2)

---

## C

**C/A (areal capacitance):** ε₀·3.9/EOT; 13.8 fF/µm² at EOT 2.5 nm. (Ch. 1.4.1)

**CDE (chemical downstream etch):** Remote-plasma F etch with no ions; one bottle method. (Ch. 13.2.2)

**Coburn–Winters:** Long-tube transport, Γ_bot/Γ_top ≈ 1/(1 + (3/4)sA). (Ch. 4.3.2)

**Collar:** Thick oxide (20–25 nm) on the upper trench (0.10–1.20 µm) that suppresses the vertical parasitic transistor. (Ch. 1.2.1, 13.1)

**C_s:** Storage capacitance; 20.6 fF reference, ≥ 19.0 fF floor. (Ch. 1.4)

---

## D

**DT (deep trench):** The capacitor trench etched into the silicon substrate. (Ch. 1)

**DT-R1:** Reference HBr/NF₃/O₂ ICP etch at +60 °C; ER₀ 1600 nm/min; three steps, 344 s. (Ch. 3.3, 8.2)

**DT-R2:** Cryogenic SF₆/O₂ etch at −100 °C; ER₀ 3000 nm/min; 150 s. (Ch. 3.7, 7)

**∂h/∂w:** Depth change per nm of CD at fixed time; 26.0 nm/nm (R1), 20.8 (R2). (Ch. 4.5)

---

## E

**eDRAM (embedded DRAM):** DRAM built on a logic process; the main surviving user of the trench cell. (Ch. 1.7, 14.1)

**Edge tilt:** Inclination of ions near the wafer edge from sheath curvature over the edge ring. (Ch. 11.2)

**EOT (equivalent oxide thickness):** 2.50 nm reference node dielectric. (Ch. 1.4.1)

**ER₀:** Open-feature reference rate in the lumped model; 1600 nm/min (R1). (Ch. 4.3.3)

**EWMA:** Exponentially weighted moving average feedback on ER₀, λ = 0.3. (Ch. 15.4)

---

## F

**Facet:** 45° erosion of the BSG mask corner; reflects ions into the upper trench. (Ch. 2.4)

**Feed-forward:** Adjusting ME3 time from measured post-open CD: −2.6 s per nm. (Ch. 15.3)

**Flamm rate:** Spontaneous F etch of Si: R = 2.91×10⁻¹² T^½ n_F exp(−1250/T) Å/min. (Ch. 3.2.1)

---

## G

**Grass:** Silicon needles at the trench bottom from micromasks. (Ch. 12.1)

**γ_O, γ_F:** Wall recombination probabilities for O and F; change with wall coating. (Ch. 9.1)

---

## I

**ICP (inductively coupled plasma):** Decoupled source/bias architecture used for DT-R1 and DT-R2. (Ch. 5)

**IR depth:** Mid-infrared reflectometry of the trench layer as an effective medium. (Ch. 15.2.1)

---

## M

**ME1 / ME2 / ME3:** Main-etch steps for 0–1.2, 1.2–3.5, 3.5–5.0 µm; 54, 154, 136 s. (Ch. 8.2)

**Merging:** Loss of the silicon wall between neighbouring trenches; node-to-node short. (Ch. 11)

**MERIE:** Magnetically enhanced RIE; early trench-etch platform. (Ch. 5.1)

**Micromask:** Small slow-etching patch on the etch front that seeds grass. (Ch. 12.1.1)

---

## N

**N-band:** Buried n-type layer connecting all buried plates. (Ch. 1.2.1)

**Neck:** Narrowing at the trench mouth; seals the fill first and leaves voids. (Ch. 10.3)

**Node dielectric:** Dielectric between storage node and buried plate. (Ch. 1.2.1)

**Notch:** Lateral etch below the BOX from charging of its insulating sidewall. (Ch. 14.1.3)

---

## O

**OES:** Optical emission spectroscopy; SiBr, O/Ar, F/Ar for rate and wall state. (Ch. 9.1.2, 15.5)

---

## P

**Pad SiN:** 150 nm nitride under the mask; CMP stop and later recess mask; must survive intact. (Ch. 2.1.2)

**Passivation film:** SiOₓBrᵧ (R1) or SiOₓFᵧ (R2) on the walls that blocks lateral F etching. (Ch. 3.4, 7.1)

---

## R

**Recess 1/2/3:** Poly recess etches to 1.20 µm, ≈ 120 nm, ≈ 50 nm below the surface. (Ch. 14.2)

**Retention time:** t_ret = C_s ΔV_allow / I_leak; ≈ 72 s typical. (Ch. 16.1)

---

## S

**Seasoning:** Controlled SiOₓBrᵧ deposition at the end of the WAC so every wafer starts on the same wall. (Ch. 9.2)

**SiBr₄:** Main by-product; ≈ 1.7 g per wafer. (Ch. 5.3)

**Silicon trench capacitor:** Passive capacitor of µm-wide, tens-of-µm-deep trenches; ≈ 69 nF/mm² single layer. (Ch. 14.3)

**S_eff (effective selectivity):** Si depth over BSG lost; 10 (R1), 40 (R2). (Ch. 2.3)

**σ_wall:** Random variation of the wall between neighbours; 1.95 nm at mid-bottle. (Ch. 11.4)

---

## T

**Taper:** Narrowing with depth; 70 → 50 nm (minor) reference. (Ch. 10.4)

**Tilt:** Collective inclination of trenches; does not reduce the wall between neighbours. (Ch. 11.1)

**Twist:** Random departure of an individual trench; σ ≈ 2 nm at 5 µm; spends the wall. (Ch. 11.3)

---

## V

**Virtual metrology:** Depth predicted from OES, RF, and chamber data; ±35 nm (3σ). (Ch. 15.5)

**Voltage contrast:** E-beam inspection after fill that finds blocked or shallow trenches. (Ch. 12.4.3)

---

## W

**WAC (waferless auto-clean):** 60 s NF₃/O₂ + O₂ + seasoning after each wafer. (Ch. 9.2)

**Wall (silicon):** Silicon between neighbouring trenches; 110 nm at the top, 70 nm at mid-bottle, ≥ 40 nm required. (Ch. 1.3.3, 11.4)
